My Dearest,

Happy Birthday!

I don’t even know how to start this properly… because every time I try to put what I feel for you into words, it never feels enough.

This year with you, changed me.

You came into my life playful, teasing, always pulling my leg… and somehow, without even trying, you became my safest place.

I love how you laugh at me.

I love how you act mature when I least expect it.

I love how you pretend to be strong even when you’ve been through things that would break most people.

And I need you to hear this clearly —

With me, you will never have to beg for love.

You will never have to prove you’re enough.

You will never have to feel like you’re too much.

I choose you.

Not because it’s easy.

Not because it’s convenient.

But because when I look at you, I see someone I don’t want to lose.

You don’t just matter to me.

You matter deeply.

On your birthday, I’m not just celebrating you turning a year older.

I’m celebrating the fact that I get to love you.

And I don’t say that lightly.

I love you. More than I probably show sometimes. More than I even know how to explain.

I love you.

And as long as I’m here, you will always be chosen.

Always mine.

Always safe.

Always us.
